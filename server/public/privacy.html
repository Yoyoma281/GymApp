DojoFit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

The short version: DojoFit has no accounts and collects no personal information. It stores nothing about you on our servers beyond anonymous counts of which sports and drills get opened — and you can switch even that off inside the app.

What the app collects

DojoFit does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or health data, and it has no login. The app works offline; its catalogue of sports, drills and exercises is bundled inside the app itself.

If anonymous usage statistics are enabled (they can be turned off in the app under Credits & licenses → Privacy), the app sends:

  • a random identifier generated on your device, which is not linked to you or to any account and is deleted when you uninstall the app;
  • which screens were opened — for example that a sport or drill was viewed, or that a video was played;
  • the platform (Android, iOS or web) and the app version.

These are used only to see which content is worth expanding. They are not sold, shared, or used for advertising or profiling.

Data the app requests from other services

To show exercise demonstrations and photographs, the app loads media from third-party services. Those services receive the technical information any web request carries, such as your IP address, and handle it under their own privacy policies:

  • MuscleWiki — exercise demonstration videos
  • wger — exercise images and muscle diagrams
  • Pexels — sport clips
  • Vercel — hosts the app's small backend

Opening a “Find tutorials” link leaves the app and takes you to YouTube, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

DojoFit is a general-audience fitness reference. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Anonymous usage statistics can be turned off at any time in Credits & licenses → Privacy. Because nothing is tied to an identity, there is no account to delete; uninstalling the app removes the random identifier and any cached media from your device.
